Losing 5% to 10% of body weight improves metabolic control

February 5, 2026 1 min read

Evidence gathered across several type 2 diabetes updates agrees on a clear message: people who manage to lose between 5% and 10% of their body weight, combining a healthy eating plan with proper portions and a regular exercise program, tend to achieve better metabolic control.

This kind of lifestyle change has been linked not only to improved glucose levels, but also to better blood pressure and a more favorable lipid profile — two key factors in preventing cardiovascular complications.

Specialists note that these goals are more sustainable when approached gradually and paired with professional follow-up, rather than short-term restrictive diets.

Original source: Elsevier - Endocrinología, Diabetes y Nutrición
